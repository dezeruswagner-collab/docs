This FAQ covers the most common questions players ask about GameLearn. Whether you're just getting started or you've been playing for a while, you'll find fast, clear answers below — no scrolling through forums required.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is GameLearn free to play?">
    Yes — every game on GameLearn is completely free to play. There's no subscription, no paywall, and no hidden charges. Just show up and start playing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need an account to play?">
    You can browse the game library and jump into any game without creating an account. However, saving your scores, tracking achievements, and climbing the leaderboards all require a free GameLearn account. Sign up takes less than a minute.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset my password?">
    Head to the login page and click **Forgot Password**. Enter the email address linked to your account and we'll send you a reset link. Follow the link in that email to set a new password and get back in the game.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why isn't a game loading?">
    A few things can block a game from loading. Start here:

    - Make sure you're using a supported browser — **Chrome**, **Firefox**, or **Edge** are recommended.
    - Disable browser extensions (especially ad blockers or script blockers) and refresh the page.
    - Check your internet connection and confirm you can reach other sites normally.

    For a full step-by-step fix, see the [Troubleshooting guide](/help/troubleshooting).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I report a bug or issue?">
    You can report any bug or issue in two ways:

    - Tap the **Feedback** button found inside any game to send a quick in-game report.
    - Email the support team directly at **support@gamelearn.gg** with your username, browser, and a description of what happened.

    The team reviews every report, so don't hold back — your feedback helps make GameLearn better for everyone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I play on mobile?">
    Absolutely! GameLearn is fully optimized for mobile browsers. Tap controls automatically replace keyboard inputs, so the experience feels native on any smartphone or tablet. No app download needed — just open your mobile browser and play.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do weekly leaderboards work?">
    Weekly leaderboards reset every **Monday at 00:00 UTC**, giving every player a fresh shot at the top each week. The top 3 players at reset time earn special weekly badges added to their profile. Check out the [Leaderboards guide](/guides/leaderboards) for tips on climbing the rankings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    You can permanently delete your account by navigating to **Profile → Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account**. 

    <Warning>
      This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your scores, achievements, and progress will be erased. Make sure you're certain before proceeding.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there ads?">
    GameLearn shows minimal, non-intrusive ads **between game sessions only** — never during active gameplay. We keep the ad experience as light as possible so it doesn't get in the way of your fun.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How often are new games added?">
    New titles drop **every week**! Follow the GameLearn social media accounts to catch announcements the moment new games go live. You can also check the [Games Overview](/games/overview) page to browse the latest additions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

This guide walks through step-by-step fixes for the most common GameLearn issues. Pick the tab that matches your problem, follow the steps in order, and you'll be back in the game in no time.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Game Not Loading">
    If a game won't start or gets stuck on a loading screen, work through these steps one by one:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Hard-refresh the page">
        Force your browser to reload all assets fresh. Press **Ctrl+Shift+R** on Windows/Linux or **Cmd+Shift+R** on Mac. This clears cached files that may be causing the hang.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Disable browser extensions">
        Extensions like ad blockers, script blockers, or VPNs can interfere with game scripts. Disable all extensions temporarily, then reload the game page.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Try incognito or private mode">
        Open a new incognito (Chrome) or private (Firefox/Edge) window and navigate to the game. This rules out extension and cookie conflicts in one step.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Switch to Chrome or Firefox">
        GameLearn is best supported on **Chrome**, **Firefox**, and **Edge**. If you're on a different browser, switching often resolves loading failures immediately.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check if GameLearn is reachable">
        Visit [https://status.gamelearn.gg](https://status.gamelearn.gg) to see if there's a known outage or ongoing maintenance. If the site is down, sit tight — the team will have it back up shortly.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Score Not Saving">
    Finished a game and your score didn't stick? Run through these checks:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Confirm you are logged in">
        Look for your **avatar icon** in the top-right corner of the page. If you don't see it, you're not logged in — scores only save to registered accounts. Log in and replay the game.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check that cookies are enabled">
        GameLearn uses cookies to keep your session active and sync your scores. Open your browser settings and make sure cookies are enabled for **gamelearn.gg**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Finish the game fully before closing the tab">
        Scores are submitted at the official game-over screen. Closing the tab mid-session or navigating away before that screen will cause your score to be lost.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Contact support if the score is still missing">
        If you completed the game, were logged in, and the score still didn't appear, email **support@gamelearn.gg** with your username, the game name, and the approximate time you played. The team can investigate your session logs.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Login Issues">
    Can't get into your account? Try these steps in order:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Reset your password">
        Go to the login page and click **Forgot Password**. Enter your account email and follow the reset link we send you. This resolves the vast majority of login failures.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your spam folder">
        Password reset and verification emails sometimes land in spam or promotions folders. Search your inbox for **gamelearn.gg** if you don't see it arrive within a few minutes.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Try a different browser">
        Browser-specific issues — like corrupted session storage — can block logins. Open Chrome or Firefox and try logging in from there to rule this out.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Clear cookies and retry">
        Old or corrupted cookies can interfere with authentication. Clear cookies and site data for **gamelearn.gg** in your browser settings, then attempt to log in again.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Slow Performance">
    If games are lagging or stuttering, these steps can get things running smoothly again:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Close unused browser tabs">
        Each open tab consumes memory and CPU. Close everything you don't need before launching a game — even a few extra tabs can make a noticeable difference.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Lower graphics quality in game settings">
        Most GameLearn titles include a **Settings** or **Quality** menu accessible from the in-game pause screen. Dropping the graphics quality one notch often eliminates lag on lower-end devices.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your network speed">
        Run a quick speed test at a site like fast.com. GameLearn games need a stable connection — inconsistent or slow speeds cause stuttering even if your hardware is fine.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Try a wired connection instead of Wi-Fi">
        Wi-Fi can introduce latency and packet loss, especially on crowded networks. If possible, plug directly into your router with an Ethernet cable for the most stable experience.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Still Need Help? Contact Support

If you've worked through the steps above and are still running into trouble, the GameLearn support team is ready to help. Send an email to **support@gamelearn.gg** and include the following details so the team can resolve your issue as quickly as possible:

- Your **GameLearn username**
- The **browser and version** you're using (e.g., Chrome 124)
- A clear **description of the issue**, including any error messages you see

The team typically responds within one business day.

<Note>
  Experiencing a widespread issue? Check the **GameLearn Status Page** at [https://status.gamelearn.gg](https://status.gamelearn.gg) for real-time updates on known issues, ongoing incidents, and scheduled maintenance. If something is already listed there, the team is already on it.
</Note>

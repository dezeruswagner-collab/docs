Getting started on GameLearn is fast — you can go from the home page to playing your first game in under five minutes. This guide walks you through creating your account, finding a game, and making sure your progress gets saved so every session counts.

<Tip>
  Not ready to sign up yet? You can browse the entire Game Library and read all the guides without an account. Just head to [/games/overview](/games/overview) to start exploring — no login required.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Go to [gamelearn.gg](https://gamelearn.gg) and click **Sign Up** in the top-right corner. Fill in your chosen username, email address, and a password. Once you confirm your email, your account is ready to go. Your username is how other players will see you on leaderboards and in the community, so pick one you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Your First Game">
    Head to the [Game Library](/games/overview) to browse all available titles. You can filter by genre — Action, Puzzle, Strategy, or Arcade — or type a game name into the search bar if you already have something in mind. Each game card shows you the title, genre, and a quick description so you can find the right fit fast.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Playing">
    Found something you want to try? Click **Play** on the game card and it launches directly in your browser. No downloads, no plugins, no waiting. If you want a head start, check out the game's guide before you dive in — you'll find a link on every game's detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Progress">
    As long as you're logged in, GameLearn automatically saves your scores and tracks your achievements after every session. Visit your [Player Profile](/profile/progress) to see your play history, total points, and the achievements you've unlocked. The more you play, the more your profile grows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once you've played your first game, there's plenty more to explore. Check out the Game Library to discover new genres, or head to the Achievements page to see all the milestones you can chase.

## Jump In Fast

New to GameLearn? You can go from zero to playing your first game in just three steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Head to [gamelearn.gg](https://gamelearn.gg) and sign up with a username, email, and password. It only takes a minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the Library">
    Explore the Game Library to find titles by genre, popularity, or your own taste. Use the search bar to find something specific, or just browse until something catches your eye.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Playing">
    Click **Play** on any game card and jump straight in. Your scores and achievements are saved automatically once you're logged in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Already hooked? Head over to the [Achievements](/profile/achievements) page to see every badge and milestone you can unlock — and start chasing them.
</Tip>

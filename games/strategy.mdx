## Strategy Sub-Genres on GameLearn

The strategy category covers a wide spectrum of gameplay styles. Here's what you'll find across the collection:

**Turn-Based Strategy** — Take your time, analyze every option, and execute your plan one deliberate move at a time. Turn-based titles like Battle Grid put full control of the pace in your hands.

**Real-Time Strategy (RTS)** — Manage units, resources, and objectives simultaneously as the clock keeps ticking. RTS games test how well you prioritize under constant pressure — and reward you when you nail it.

**Resource Management** — Games like Supply Chain challenge you to balance input, output, and demand across complex systems. Efficiency is your greatest weapon, and every optimized route feels like a win.

**Empire Building** — Start small and scale up. Empire builders like Kingdom Rise and Conquest Zero reward long-term planning and bold strategic investment over quick wins.

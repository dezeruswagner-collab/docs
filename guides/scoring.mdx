Every game on GameLearn tracks your performance through a live score counter, and the way points stack up depends on the genre you're playing. Mastering how scoring works — and what each genre rewards — is the key to climbing the leaderboards and setting personal bests that actually mean something. Here's everything you need to know about the GameLearn scoring system.

## Base Scoring

Every title on GameLearn displays a score counter while you play. Points accumulate based on in-game actions: defeating enemies, solving puzzles, making smart decisions, or simply surviving. The exact formula varies by game, but the core principle is always the same — play well, score higher.

Your score resets at the start of each new session. At the end of a session, GameLearn captures your final score and compares it against your personal best. If you beat your record, your new high score is saved automatically — as long as you're logged in.

## Scoring by Genre

Different genres reward different skills. Here's how scoring works across the GameLearn catalog:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Action">
    Action games reward speed and style. Your score climbs faster when you chain attacks together without stopping — the longer your combo, the higher your **combo multiplier**. Letting your combo drop resets the multiplier back to 1×, so keeping up the pressure is essential.

    - **Speed bonus** — finish levels or objectives quickly to earn time-based score bonuses
    - **Combo multiplier** — chain consecutive hits or actions to multiply all points earned during the combo
    - **Enemy difficulty** — tougher enemies are worth more base points than weaker ones

    The highest action scores come from players who stay aggressive and keep their combo alive from start to finish.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Puzzle">
    Puzzle games punish inefficiency and reward clever thinking. Your score is calculated based on how close your solution comes to the **minimum number of moves** required to solve the puzzle — the fewer moves you use, the higher your efficiency score.

    - **Move efficiency** — using fewer moves than average boosts your score significantly; exceeding the minimum costs you points
    - **Time bonus** — solve the puzzle quickly to earn an additional time-based bonus on top of your efficiency score
    - **Hint penalty** — using hints deducts points from your final score, so try to resist the temptation

    The best puzzle scores come from players who plan their moves before executing them.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Strategy">
    Strategy games score you on how decisively and efficiently you achieve victory. Winning matters, but *how* you win matters just as much.

    - **Strategic efficiency** — completing objectives with fewer resources or turns earns bonus points
    - **Victory margin** — the bigger your lead over the opponent or target when you win, the higher your score multiplier
    - **Speed of victory** — finishing faster than the average time for that scenario adds a time-efficiency bonus

    Dragging out a match or wasting resources will cost you points even if you win — strategy games reward players who think ahead and execute cleanly.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Arcade">
    Arcade games keep it classic: survive longer, score higher. There are no complex multipliers or efficiency calculations — just pure, escalating point accumulation.

    - **Survival time** — the longer you stay alive or in play, the more points you rack up
    - **Milestone bonuses** — passing certain score thresholds or stages triggers bonus point drops
    - **Difficulty scaling** — as the game speeds up or gets harder over time, each action you complete is worth more points

    Arcade high scores are all about consistency and resilience. One bad run can cost everything, so steady play almost always beats risky plays.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## High Scores and Leaderboards

GameLearn tracks two types of scores for every game you play:

- **Personal Best** — your highest score ever on a specific game, displayed on the game detail page and your profile
- **Global Leaderboard** — a ranked list of the top scores across all GameLearn players for each game

Your personal best updates automatically whenever you beat your previous record. Global leaderboard rankings refresh in real time, so you'll see exactly where you stand against the community the moment your score is submitted.

<Note>
  Scores only save when you're logged in to your GameLearn account. If you play as a guest, your score won't be recorded or counted toward the leaderboard. [Set up your account](/profile/account-setup) to make every run count.
</Note>

Challenges are GameLearn's built-in quest system — a constantly refreshing set of objectives designed to push your skills, mix up your routine, and reward you generously for stepping outside your comfort zone. Every day brings a new set of bite-sized missions, and every week a bigger tournament-style challenge drops for players who are ready to go all in. Completing challenges is the fastest way to earn XP, stack up coins, and unlock achievements you can't get anywhere else.

## Daily Challenges

Every day, GameLearn offers **three daily challenges** across different game genres. They reset at **midnight UTC** — so there's always a fresh batch waiting for you when you log in.

Daily challenges are designed to be completable in a single sitting and cover a wide range of objectives. Here are some examples of the types of challenges you'll encounter:

- *"Score 500 points in any arcade game"*
- *"Complete a puzzle level without using any hints"*
- *"Survive for 3 minutes in an action game"*
- *"Win a strategy match in under 10 minutes"*

Each daily challenge rewards you with **challenge coins** and **XP** on completion. Finishing all three in a single day adds a bonus multiplier to your daily XP total — so it pays to be consistent.

## Weekly Challenges

Weekly challenges are bigger, bolder objectives that span a full **7-day window**. They reset every Monday alongside the Weekly Leaderboard rankings and offer substantially higher rewards than daily challenges.

Weekly challenges often require sustained effort across multiple sessions — for example, accumulating a total score across several games, completing a series of puzzle levels, or achieving a specific milestone in a strategy game. The objectives push you further than any single daily challenge, but the rewards match the effort: large XP bonuses, rare achievement badges, and bonus challenge coins that aren't available anywhere else.

## How to Find and Start a Challenge

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Challenges in the Nav">
    From anywhere on GameLearn, click **Challenges** in the main navigation bar. This opens the Challenges hub, where all active daily and weekly challenges are displayed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse Active Challenges">
    Scroll through the active challenge list. Each challenge card shows the objective, the reward, the time remaining, and whether you've already accepted or completed it. Use the tabs at the top to switch between **Daily** and **Weekly** views.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Accept">
    Find a challenge you want to tackle and click the **Accept** button on its card. Accepting a challenge pins it to your active challenge tracker so you can monitor your progress in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Objective In-Game">
    Launch the relevant game and play normally — GameLearn automatically tracks your progress toward the challenge objective as you play. When you hit the target, the challenge completes and your rewards land in your account immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Challenge Rewards

Completing challenges earns you three types of rewards:

- **Challenge Coins** — a special currency earned exclusively through challenges, redeemable in the GameLearn rewards store for profile customizations and power-ups
- **XP Boosts** — bonus experience points that accelerate your level progression and unlock profile milestones faster
- **Exclusive Achievement Badges** — unique badges tied to specific challenge milestones that can't be earned through regular gameplay; these show up on your public profile and leaderboard entries

The more challenges you complete — and the more consistently you do them day after day — the faster your coins and XP compound into meaningful rewards.

<Tip>
  Not sure you can complete a challenge on your first try? Use **Practice Mode** in the relevant game to get comfortable with the objective before accepting the challenge. Practice Mode doesn't count toward challenge progress, so you can rehearse as many times as you need without burning your attempt. Check out the [Advanced Tips guide](/guides/advanced-tips) for more on getting the most out of Practice Mode.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Challenges require a **logged-in GameLearn account** to track and complete. If you play as a guest, your progress won't be recorded and you won't receive any rewards. [Set up your account](/profile/account-setup) to make every challenge count.
</Note>

GameLearn's leaderboard system turns every game into a competition. Whether you're chasing the global top 100, keeping tabs on friends, or gunning for a weekly badge, the leaderboards give your scores real meaning and keep the rivalry alive. Every point you earn is a chance to climb — here's how it all works.

## Global Leaderboard

The Global Leaderboard tracks the **top 100 players** for every game on GameLearn. Scores update in real time, so the moment you submit a new personal best, your ranking reflects it instantly — no waiting for a daily refresh.

Each entry on the global leaderboard shows the player's username, their high score, and when they set it. Use it to gauge where the skill ceiling really is for a game, identify the score you need to crack the top 10, and see how the competition stacks up. Only your all-time best score counts toward your global ranking — so every run is a chance to improve your standing.

## Friends Leaderboard

The Friends Leaderboard filters the rankings to show only the players you follow on GameLearn. This makes it easy to run a personal rivalry with your crew without getting lost in a pool of thousands of global competitors.

Your friends' scores update in real time alongside yours. If a friend beats your score, you'll see their name jump above yours immediately — perfect motivation to fire up another run. Following more players on GameLearn enriches your Friends Leaderboard and keeps the competition dynamic and personal. You can manage who you follow from your [profile](/profile/progress).

## Weekly Rankings

Every week, GameLearn runs a separate Weekly Rankings competition that resets every **Monday at midnight UTC**. Your weekly rank is based solely on scores submitted during that 7-day window — your all-time high score doesn't carry over.

Weekly Rankings add a fresh competitive layer: even if you're not in the global top 100, you have a real shot at the weekly podium by having a great week. The top 3 players on each game's weekly ranking earn **special weekly badges** added to their profile. These badges are exclusive to weekly competition winners and can't be earned any other way.

## How to View a Game's Leaderboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Any Game">
    Navigate to [Games Overview](/games/overview) and click on any game to open its detail page. You can also jump directly to a genre — [Action](/games/action), [Puzzle](/games/puzzle), [Strategy](/games/strategy), or [Arcade](/games/arcade) — and pick from there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Trophy Icon">
    On the game detail page, click the **trophy icon** located near the game title or in the top-right corner of the game panel. This opens the leaderboard overlay for that specific game.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch Between Tabs">
    The leaderboard overlay has three tabs at the top: **Global**, **Friends**, and **Weekly**. Click any tab to switch views. Your own ranking highlights automatically so you can see exactly where you stand in each category at a glance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Leaderboard entries require a **verified GameLearn account**. Guest plays and unverified accounts don't appear on any leaderboard, and scores submitted without being logged in aren't counted. [Set up and verify your account](/profile/account-setup) to get your scores on the board.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Leaderboard success unlocks exclusive achievements you can't earn any other way. Visit your [Achievements page](/profile/achievements) to see which leaderboard milestones are available and track how close you are to unlocking the next one.
</Tip>

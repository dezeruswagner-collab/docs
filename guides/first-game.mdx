Welcome to GameLearn! Whether you're a seasoned gamer or picking up a controller for the first time, getting started is fast, easy, and a whole lot of fun. This guide walks you through everything you need to find a game you'll love, launch it, and start racking up points — no experience required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the Library">
    Head over to [Games Overview](/games/overview) to explore the full GameLearn catalog. Filter by genre — Action, Puzzle, Strategy, or Arcade — to zero in on the type of game that sounds most exciting to you. Not sure where to start? Try browsing a couple of genres and reading the short descriptions to get a feel for what's available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Game">
    Click any game card to open its detail page. Here you'll find a full description of the game, its genre tag, difficulty rating, average player rating, and estimated play time. Take a moment to read through the details so you know what you're jumping into before you commit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch the Game">
    Ready to play? Hit the big **Play** button on the game detail page. The game loads right in your browser — no downloads or installs needed. Sit back for a moment while the loading screen does its thing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the Controls">
    Every game on GameLearn displays a controls cheat sheet on the loading screen. Give it a quick read before you dive in. You can also pull up the controls guide at any time from the in-game pause menu. Knowing your controls from the start makes a huge difference in your first run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Have Fun">
    Play, explore, and experiment! If you're logged in to your GameLearn account, your score saves automatically at the end of each session. You can track your personal bests and progress over time — so every game you play counts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's on a Game Card

Game cards give you a snapshot of everything you need to decide if a game is right for you. Here's what each card shows at a glance:

- **Genre Tag** — the category the game belongs to (Action, Puzzle, Strategy, or Arcade)
- **Difficulty** — a simple rating that tells you how challenging the game is for new players
- **Player Rating** — an average score from the GameLearn community, so you know what other players think
- **Estimated Play Time** — how long a typical session lasts, so you can pick a game that fits your schedule

Use these details together to find a game that matches your skill level and how much time you have. A quick 5-minute arcade game hits differently than a deep strategy title that rewards longer sessions.

<Tip>
  If you're brand new to GameLearn, arcade games are the perfect starting point. They're fast, intuitive, and designed for instant fun — no long tutorials required. Check out the [Arcade Games](/games/arcade) section to find your first game.
</Tip>

<Note>
  GameLearn runs best on the latest versions of **Chrome**, **Firefox**, and **Edge**. Mobile browsers are fully supported, so you can play on the go from your phone or tablet without missing a beat.
</Note>

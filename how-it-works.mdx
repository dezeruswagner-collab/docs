GameLearn is built around a simple loop: find a game you want to play, play it, earn points and achievements, and watch your profile grow over time. Everything on the platform — the Genre Library, the Player Profile, the guides — is designed to keep that loop smooth and rewarding. Here's a closer look at how it all fits together.

<Note>
  You don't need an account to browse the Game Library or read guides. However, you do need to sign in to save your progress, track scores, and unlock achievements.
</Note>

## The Core GameLearn Loop

Every session on GameLearn follows the same satisfying rhythm. Browse, pick, play, earn, and repeat.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the Genre Library">
    Start on the [Game Library](/games/overview) page and explore titles organized by genre: Action, Puzzle, Strategy, and Arcade. Filter by popularity, newest additions, or genre to find your next favorite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Game">
    Click any game card to see its details — description, genre tags, difficulty level, and a link to its guide. When something looks good, hit **Play** to launch it instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play">
    Games run directly in your browser — no downloads, no installs. Jump in and start playing right away. Each game comes with a guide if you want tips before or after your session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn Points and Achievements">
    Every game session earns you points. Hit certain milestones — high scores, play streaks, first completions — and you'll unlock achievements that show up on your Player Profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Your Progress">
    Head to your [Profile](/profile/progress) to review your play history, check your achievement collection, and see what's next on your gaming journey.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Genre Library

The Genre Library is the heart of GameLearn. It organizes every title on the platform into four clear categories so you can always find what you're in the mood for.

- **Action** — Reflex-driven games with fast gameplay and high energy.
- **Puzzle** — Logic and creativity challenges at your own pace.
- **Strategy** — Deep gameplay where smart decisions lead to victory.
- **Arcade** — Short, score-chasing sessions built for replay value.

Each genre page highlights featured games, top-rated titles, and links to relevant guides. You can also use the search bar at the top of any page to find a specific game by name or tag.

## The Player Profile System

Your Player Profile is your personal scoreboard. Once you create an account, GameLearn automatically records every game you play, every score you earn, and every achievement you unlock.

Key profile features include:

- **Play History** — A log of every game session with timestamps and scores.
- **Achievements** — Badges earned by hitting milestones in specific games or across the platform.
- **Points Total** — A running tally of all the points you've earned, updated after every session.
- **Favorite Games** — Pin the games you play most so they're always one click away.

<Info>
  You can customize your Player Profile with a display name and avatar from the [Account Setup](/profile/account-setup) page. Make it yours.
</Info>

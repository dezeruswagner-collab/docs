GameLearn doesn't just let you play games — it keeps the receipts. Every session you jump into, every score you post, and every milestone you hit gets recorded automatically and displayed on your personal progress dashboard. Whether you want to see how far you've come, challenge your own best scores, or prove to yourself that 7-day streak really happened, your progress page has the full story.

## Your Progress Dashboard

Your **Progress Dashboard** is the command center for everything you've done on GameLearn. Access it by clicking your avatar in the top-right corner and selecting **Progress**.

### Recent Games Played

The **Recent Games** section shows the last games you've jumped into, along with:
- The **date and time** of each session
- Your **score** for that session
- A quick-jump button to **play again** instantly

Your 20 most recent sessions are always visible. Scroll down to load older history.

### Total Play Time

GameLearn tracks how long you've been playing — across all games and all sessions. Your **Total Play Time** counter appears at the top of your dashboard and updates in real time. Use it as a badge of honor or a reminder to take a snack break.

### High Scores Per Game

Every game on GameLearn maintains its own **personal high score** for your account. Your dashboard lists:
- The **game name** and genre
- Your **all-time best score** for that game
- Your **rank** on the global leaderboard for that score

Beat your own record and the dashboard updates instantly — no page refresh needed.

## Milestones

Milestones are automatic markers that celebrate key moments in your GameLearn journey. They're not the same as achievements (which require specific actions) — milestones happen naturally as you keep playing.

Here are some milestones you'll hit along the way:

- 🎮 **Played 10 Games** — You've explored 10 different games in the library
- 🏆 **First High Score** — You posted your very first personal best
- 🔥 **7-Day Streak** — You played at least one game every day for a week straight
- ⏱️ **1 Hour Played** — You've racked up a full hour of total play time
- 🌟 **Tried Every Genre** — You've played at least one game from each available genre
- 🚀 **Top 100** — You cracked the global top 100 on any leaderboard

### How to View Your Milestones

Navigate to **Profile → Progress → Milestones** to see every milestone you've earned, with the date you hit each one. Milestones you haven't reached yet appear grayed out so you always know what's coming next.

## Resetting Your Progress

Sometimes you want a fresh start — no judgment here. GameLearn gives you two reset options:

### Per-Game Reset

Wipe the slate clean for a single game without touching anything else. This removes:
- Your session history for that game
- Your personal high score for that game
- Any milestones tied exclusively to that game

To reset a single game: go to **Progress → Recent Games**, click the **⋯** menu next to the game, and select **Reset Progress for This Game**.

### Full Progress Reset

A full reset wipes your entire progress history — all session records, all high scores, and all milestones. Your account itself, your avatar, and your achievements **are not affected** by a full reset.

To perform a full reset: go to **Profile → Settings → Data** and select **Reset All Progress**. You'll be asked to confirm twice before anything is deleted. This action cannot be undone.

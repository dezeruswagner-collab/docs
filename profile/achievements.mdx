Achievements are GameLearn's way of cheering you on as you play. They're collectible badges that recognize everything from bold exploration to laser-focused high scoring — proof that you showed up, played hard, and left your mark. Whether you're the type to hunt every last one or you just love the dopamine hit when a new badge pops, the achievement system gives every session a little extra meaning.

## Achievement Categories

GameLearn's achievements are organized into four categories. Each one rewards a different style of play, so there's something for every type of gamer.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Explorer">
    **Explorer achievements** reward you for getting out there and trying new things. The more of the GameLearn library you discover, the more badges you unlock.

    These achievements track:
    - The total number of **unique games** you've played
    - Playing games across **multiple genres**
    - Visiting specific sections of the game library like [Action](/games/action), [Puzzle](/games/puzzle), and [Strategy](/games/strategy)

    Explorer achievements are perfect for players who love variety and want to see everything GameLearn has to offer.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="High Scorer">
    **High Scorer achievements** are for the competitors — the players who aren't satisfied until they've posted the best number possible.

    These achievements track:
    - Beating your own **personal best** score in any game
    - Cracking the **top 50 or top 10** on a global leaderboard
    - Achieving a **perfect score** on a game that supports it
    - Posting high scores across **multiple different games**

    Check the [Leaderboards](/guides/leaderboards) guide to sharpen your strategy before going for the big ones.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Streak Master">
    **Streak Master achievements** reward consistency. Come back day after day and GameLearn notices.

    These achievements track:
    - Playing on **consecutive days** (3-day, 7-day, 30-day streaks)
    - Logging in and playing at least one game every day of a **calendar week**
    - Recovering and **restarting a streak** after breaking one

    Your current streak is always visible at the top of your Progress Dashboard. Don't let it die — set a daily reminder if you have to.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Challenge Champion">
    **Challenge Champion achievements** are the rarest and most rewarding badges on GameLearn. They're earned by completing limited-time and skill-based challenges.

    These achievements track:
    - Completing a **Daily Challenge**
    - Finishing a **Weekly Challenge** before the timer runs out
    - Winning a **Special Event** challenge
    - Completing challenges across **multiple game genres**

    Challenge achievements often have exclusive flair — special badge designs you can't earn any other way. Don't sleep on them.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How to View Your Achievements

Your full achievements collection lives on your profile page. Here's how to get there:

1. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner of any page
2. Select **View Profile** from the dropdown menu
3. Click the **Achievements** tab near the top of your profile page

You'll see every achievement you've earned (displayed in full color) alongside locked achievements (shown grayed out with a hint for how to unlock them). Tap any achievement to see its full description, unlock date, and how many other players have earned it.

You can also share individual achievements directly from this view — use the **Share** button to post your badge to social media or copy a link to send to friends.

<Info>
  Some achievements are **hidden** until you unlock them. If you spot a mystery badge with a "?" icon, you'll have to discover what triggers it on your own. Half the fun is the surprise.
</Info>

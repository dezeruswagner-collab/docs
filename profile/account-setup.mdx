Your GameLearn player profile is your home base — the place where every score you crush, every game you conquer, and every achievement you unlock lives together. A fully set-up profile lets you track your gaming history, climb leaderboards, and get personalized game recommendations based on the genres you love. Whether you're here to top the charts or just have fun, your profile makes the experience yours.

## Get Started: Create Your Account

Follow these steps to go from zero to fully set up in just a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Head to [gamelearn.gg/signup](https://gamelearn.gg/signup) and fill in the registration form. You'll need:
    - A valid **email address**
    - A unique **username** (this is how other players will see you)
    - A strong **password** (at least 8 characters, mix of letters and numbers recommended)

    Hit **Create Account** when you're ready, and you're almost in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from GameLearn. Click the confirmation link inside to activate your account. If you don't see it within a couple of minutes, check your spam or junk folder — it sometimes lands there. You can also request a new verification email from the login screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Avatar">
    Once you're in, head to your profile page and pick an avatar that represents you. You can:
    - Browse the **built-in avatar gallery** — dozens of characters, icons, and game-themed options to choose from
    - **Upload a custom image** from your device (square images under 5 MB work best)

    Your avatar appears next to your name on leaderboards and in game lobbies, so make it count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Favorite Genres">
    Tell GameLearn what you're into by tagging your favorite game genres — action, puzzle, strategy, arcade, and more. Your selections power the recommendation engine, so your home feed surfaces games you'll actually want to play. You can update your genre preferences any time from your profile settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Profile Settings

Once your account is live, you can fine-tune how your profile looks and behaves. Find all of these options under **Profile → Settings**.

### Display Name

Your display name is the friendly label shown on your profile page — it's separate from your username and can include spaces, capitalization, and special characters. Change it as often as you like with no restrictions.

### Privacy Settings

Control who can see your profile and activity:

- **Public profile** — Anyone on GameLearn can view your scores, play history, and achievements.
- **Private profile** — Only you can see your full profile details. Other players will still see your username on leaderboards, but your profile page won't be publicly accessible.

Toggle between public and private at any time from the **Privacy** section of your settings.

### Email Notifications

Choose which emails you want to receive from GameLearn:

- **New game alerts** — Get notified when a new game drops in your favorite genres
- **Achievement unlocked** — Celebrate every milestone with a notification
- **Weekly recap** — A summary of your play time, top scores, and new achievements each week
- **Challenge reminders** — Stay on top of limited-time challenges before they expire

You can turn individual notification types on or off independently — no all-or-nothing toggles here.

<Note>
  You can change your **username** once every **30 days**. Choose wisely! To update it, go to **Profile → Settings → Account** and select **Change Username**. Your old username becomes available to other players after the change.
</Note>
